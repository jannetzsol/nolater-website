Subscriptions & purchases

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → No Later and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or free trial.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You won't be charged today. We remind you before the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before. The monthly plan and the lifetime purchase have no trial.

I bought Pro, but the app doesn't show it.

Open No Later, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders

My reminders don't arrive.

  • Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → No Later and make sure notifications are allowed.
  • Check that a Focus mode (like Do Not Disturb or Sleep) isn't silencing No Later.
  • Make sure the task has a deadline and is marked as urgent if you want escalating reminders.
