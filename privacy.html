2. The app

No account. You can use No Later without signing up. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number in the app.

Your tasks stay on your device. Tasks, deadlines and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no access to them.

Feedback and ratings. If you send feedback from the app, your mail app opens and you decide what to send. Rating prompts are shown by Apple.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. You can turn them off anytime in iPhone Settings.

Calendar (optional). If you turn on calendar sync, No Later asks for access to Apple Calendar and adds a calendar entry for each task deadline (titled "Deadline: …"). To update or remove these entries later, iOS requires full calendar access; No Later only reads the entries it created itself and never reads your other events. Everything happens on your device. You can revoke access anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Widget. The widget shows your current Focus task on your Home Screen. Its data stays on your device.

3. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

To manage subscriptions and unlock Pro, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that is not linked to your name, your purchase and subscription information (for example product, price, renewal and trial status), the App Store country and basic technical data (device type, iOS version, app version, IP address). This is necessary to provide the purchase you made (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We have a data processing agreement with RevenueCat. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. RevenueCat keeps this data as long as it is needed to provide your purchases and as long as statutory retention periods require.

4. Usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To understand which features are used and where the app can be improved (for example, how many people complete the onboarding), we use TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany). TelemetryDeck receives anonymized usage signals (for example "onboarding completed" or "task completed") together with technical data: app version and build number, whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight, iOS version, device type, language and region, time zone, screen size and accessibility settings (for example text size or "Reduce Motion"). Identifiers are hashed before they leave your device, timestamps are rounded and IP addresses are not stored. We cannot identify you from this data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

On by default, easy to turn off. Usage statistics are on when you first open the app. You can turn them off anytime in the app under Settings → "Anonymous usage statistics". From then on, no more signals are sent. Because the data is anonymous, TelemetryDeck may keep it without a fixed deletion date; it cannot be linked back to you.

No Later does not show ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell data.

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data (server logs) to deliver the site securely. Domain and DNS are provided by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The website uses no cookies and no analytics.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Emails to support@nolater.app are forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Google Gmail inbox (Google, USA). We delete your messages when they are no longer needed, unless we have to keep them by law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To exercise them, email support@nolater.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen.

Because the app works without an account, we usually can't link data to you. You can delete all task data by deleting the app.

Children. No Later is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
